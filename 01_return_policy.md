# Northwind Home Goods: Standard Return Policy

> Northwind Home Goods is a fictional retailer created for this project. These policies are sample documents, not a real company's terms.

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Electronics and small appliances have a shorter return window of 15 days from delivery. Items purchased during the holiday period (November 15 to December 24) can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## Condition requirements
Items must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories, manuals, and tags included. Items that show signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or missing parts may receive a partial refund after inspection.

## Non-returnable items
The following items are final sale and cannot be returned: gift cards, personalized or engraved items, perishable goods such as food and fresh flowers, and items marked "Final Sale" at checkout.

## How to start a return
Customers start a return from the Orders page in their account by selecting the item and a return reason. A prepaid return label is emailed within 1 hour. Returns must be shipped within 7 days of creating the return request, or the request expires.
