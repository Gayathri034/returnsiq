# Northwind Home Goods: Damaged, Defective, and Wrong Items

> Fictional sample policy created for this project.

## Reporting damage
Items that arrive damaged must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Customers must upload at least two photos showing the damage and the shipping box through the Orders page.

## Defective items
Items that stop working because of a manufacturing defect can be returned within 90 days of delivery, even after the standard return window has closed. Defective electronics are tested by the returns center before the refund is approved.

## Wrong item received
If a customer receives the wrong item, Northwind ships the correct item at no cost and emails a prepaid label for the wrong item. The customer does not need to wait for the wrong item to be returned before the correct item ships.

## No restocking fee for these cases
Restocking fees never apply to damaged, defective, or wrong items, regardless of the customer's return history.
