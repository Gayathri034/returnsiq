# Northwind Home Goods: Refunds and Exchanges

> Fictional sample policy created for this project.

## Refund timeline
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection at the returns center. Inspection usually happens within 3 business days of the package arriving.

## Store credit option
Customers can choose store credit instead of a refund. Store credit is issued immediately after inspection and includes a 5% bonus on the refunded amount. Store credit does not expire.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color of the same item are free and ship as soon as the original item is scanned by the carrier. Exchanges for a different product are processed as a return plus a new order.

## Shipping costs on refunds
Original shipping charges are refunded only when the item arrived damaged, defective, or was the wrong item. For all other returns, the original shipping charge is not refunded.
