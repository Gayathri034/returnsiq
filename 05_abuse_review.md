# Northwind Home Goods: Return Abuse Review Process

> Fictional sample policy created for this project.

## Automated flagging
An anomaly detection model reviews return behavior every week, including return rate, returns per order, average return value, and the number of different products returned. Accounts with unusual patterns are flagged for review.

## Human review required
A flag from the model never leads to an automatic penalty. Every flagged account is reviewed by a member of the Trust and Safety team, who checks order history and customer contact notes before taking any action.

## Possible outcomes
After review, the team may take no action, send an educational email about the return policy, or, for confirmed abuse such as returning a different item than the one purchased, restrict the account from making returns for 6 months.

## Appeals
Customers can appeal any restriction by contacting customer support within 30 days. Appeals are reviewed by a different team member than the one who made the original decision.
