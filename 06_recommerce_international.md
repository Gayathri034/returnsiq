# Northwind Home Goods: Returned Item Grading and International Returns

> Fictional sample policy created for this project.

## Grading returned items
Every returned item is inspected and given a grade. Grade A items are like new and are resold as Open Box at a 15% discount. Grade B items have minor cosmetic wear and are sold through the outlet store at a 35% discount. Grade C items cannot be resold and are sent to a certified recycling partner.

## International returns
Customers outside the United Kingdom can return items within the standard return window, but they pay the return shipping cost. Return shipping is free for international customers only when the item is damaged, defective, or the wrong item.

## Customs and duties
Import duties and taxes paid on international orders are not refunded by Northwind. Customers may be able to reclaim them from their local customs authority.
