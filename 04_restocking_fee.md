# Northwind Home Goods: High-Return Restocking Fee Policy

> Fictional sample policy created for this project. The thresholds mirror a scenario tested in the E-commerce Sales and Returns Analytics project.

## Who the fee applies to
A 10% restocking fee applies to returns from customers whose return rate is 20% or higher over the previous 12 months. Return rate is measured as returned value divided by purchased value.

## Minimum history
The fee only applies to customers with at least 3 orders in the previous 12 months. Customers with fewer than 3 orders are never charged the fee, because their return rate is not yet reliable.

## Exemptions
The fee never applies to damaged, defective, or wrong items. It also does not apply to exchanges for a different size or color of the same item.

## Customer notice
Customers who reach the 20% threshold receive an email notice before the fee applies to any future return. The fee is shown at the start of the return request so the customer can decide before shipping the item.

## Expected impact
In the analysis this policy is based on, a 10% fee at a 20% return-rate threshold affected about 1.1% of customers and was estimated to recover about 19,200 GBP over the period analyzed.
